Our story

Born in
Lahore

It started with one heavyweight tee and a rule: if it doesn't hold its shape after fifty washes, we don't sell it. Today designs every blank in-house — from the 240 GSM jersey to the 480 GSM brushed fleece.

We cut roomy, wash our garments before they reach you and print with water-based inks that crack and fade the way you'd expect from a favourite tee. Men, women and kids — one uniform for the whole crew.
